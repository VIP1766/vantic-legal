1. Uygulamadan sil — anında

  1. Vantic'i açın ve ana ekranın sağ üstündeki Ayarlar (dişli) simgesine dokunun.
  2. Hesabımı sil satırına dokunun.
  3. Açılan pencerede Kalıcı olarak sil'e dokunun.

Hesabınız ve aşağıda sayılan verileriniz o anda silinir; bekleme süresi yoktur. Bu işlem geri alınamaz.

2. Uygulama elinizde değilse — e-postayla

Vantic hesabınızda kayıtlı e-posta adresinizden dilber.labs@gmail.com adresine "Hesabımı sil" konulu bir e-posta gönderin. Kayıtlı adresten gelmesini istememizin sebebi, başkasının sizin adınıza silme talep edememesidir. Talebiniz en geç 30 gün içinde ve ücretsiz sonuçlandırılır ve size aynı adresten bilgi verilir.

Deleting your Vantic account

1. In the app — immediate

  1. Open Vantic and tap Settings (the gear icon, top right of the home screen).
  2. Tap Delete my account.
  3. Confirm with Delete permanently.

Your account and the data listed below are deleted immediately; there is no waiting period. This cannot be undone.

2. Without the app — by email

Email dilber.labs@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my account", from the email address registered on your Vantic account — so that nobody else can request deletion in your name. We complete the request within 30 days, free of charge, and confirm to the same address.

3. What is deleted

  • Your account and sign-in data (email, sign-in method, sessions)
  • Your analysis history, the requests (prompts) you wrote and the sections found
  • Queued analyses (cancelled)
  • Your feedback (👍/👎)
  • Your app usage records and your rows in daily reports
  • Device records used for notifications
  • Generated audio files Later

4. What remains — and why

  • Content summaries of public YouTube videos — not personal data, not linked to any user; they stay in the shared pool so other users' service is unaffected.
  • App error records — unlinked from your identity at deletion (anonymised) and removed after at most 12 months.
  • Aggregate totals in daily usage reports — not linked to anyone.
  • Purchase records Later — kept for the period tax law requires (10 years).

Subscriptions: deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play or App Store subscription — cancel it in your store settings. Later

Signing in again afterwards creates a new, empty account; your old data does not come back. Accounts unused for 24 months are deleted automatically with their data.
