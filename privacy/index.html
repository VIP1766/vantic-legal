2. YouTube API Services

Vantic uses YouTube API Services. By using Vantic you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Through the YouTube Data API we read publicly available video metadata only — title, duration, channel name, thumbnail, privacy status and live status. We use it to check, before any analysis begins, whether a video can be processed at all. We do not access your YouTube account, your watch history, your subscriptions or any private data, and we never ask for your YouTube credentials.

You can revoke Vantic's access to your data via the Google security settings page at any time.

What we never do

  • We do not download YouTube videos — neither files nor audio streams.
  • We do not sell, rent or trade your data.
  • There are no ads, no ad networks, no third-party analytics and no tracking cookies in Vantic. Your advertising ID (GAID / IDFA) is not read. App usage records stay in our own infrastructure and are never sent to an analytics or advertising company.

About the free-text box. You can describe your request in your own words. That text is sent to an AI service outside Türkiye in order to produce the analysis. 6. Security

  • Data is encrypted in transit and at rest.
  • Row-level security is enforced in the database: no user can read another user's records.
  • API keys for the AI services live only on the server and are never embedded in the app.
  • Email-like patterns in error records are automatically masked.

7. Your rights

Under KVKK article 11 you have the right to:

  • learn whether your personal data is processed, and request information about it;
  • learn the purpose of processing and whether it is used accordingly;
  • know the third parties, in Türkiye or abroad, to whom data is transferred;
  • request correction of incomplete or inaccurate data;
  • request erasure or destruction of your data;
  • request that correction and erasure be notified to those third parties;
  • object to a result reached solely by automated analysis that works against you;
  • claim compensation for damage caused by unlawful processing.

How to exercise them: write to dilber.labs@gmail.com from the email address registered on your Vantic account, stating clearly what you are asking for. We respond within 30 days, free of charge.

If your request is refused, the answer is inadequate, or you receive no answer in time, you may complain to the Turkish Data Protection Board within 30 days of learning the answer and in any case within 60 days of your original request.

Note: deleting your Vantic account does not cancel any Google Play or App Store subscription. You must cancel that in your store settings.
